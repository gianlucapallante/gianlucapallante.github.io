🎓 I'm delighted to share that as of 1 September 2026 I have joined the [Department of Economics and Management](https://www.ec.unipi.it/) of the University of Pisa as Assistant Professor in Economics.

I'm deeply grateful to the Institute of Economics and L'EMbeDS at [Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna](https://www.santannapisa.it/it), my academic home through the PhD and post-doc years, and to all the colleagues and coauthors who made this path possible.

I look forward to continuing my research on macroeconomics, innovation, and the green transition, and hopefully inspiring future students to study these topics. 🚀
